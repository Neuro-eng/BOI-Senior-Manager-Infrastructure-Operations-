High-yield notes

Linux (RHEL)
  • Package: dnf/yum, rpm. Services: systemctl. Logs: journalctl, /var/log.
  • Storage: LVM (PV, VG, LV), fdisk, mkfs, /etc/fstab. Permissions: chmod, chown, ACL, SELinux (enforcing/permissive).
  • Networking: nmcli, ip, ss, firewalld. Boot: GRUB2, targets (multi-user, graphical), root password recovery via rd.break.
Windows Server
  • AD DS, DNS, DHCP, Group Policy, FSMO roles (5), WSUS/SCCM for patching, Failover Clustering, Hyper-V.
Networking
  • OSI vs TCP/IP; subnetting; VLAN and trunking (802.1Q); STP and RSTP; EtherChannel; HSRP/VRRP.
  • OSPF (link-state, areas, Dijkstra), EIGRP (Cisco), BGP (path-vector, AS), ACLs, NAT/PAT, IPsec/SSL VPN, SNMP, NetFlow.
Storage, backup, DR
  • SAN (block, FC/iSCSI) vs NAS (file, NFS/SMB). RAID 0/1/5/6/10.
  • RPO = acceptable data loss; RTO = acceptable downtime. Hot, warm, cold DR sites. 3-2-1 backup rule. Tools: Veeam, Commvault, VMware SRM.
Virtualization, containers, cloud
  • vSphere: ESXi, vCenter, vMotion, DRS, HA, FT, datastores (VMFS).
  • Kubernetes: pod, deployment, service, ingress, node, etcd, kubelet, API server, scheduler. OpenShift adds routes, operators and built-in CI/CD on top of Kubernetes.
  • Cloud: IaaS/PaaS/SaaS; shared responsibility model; IAM; VPC/VNet; auto scaling; hybrid via VPN/ExpressRoute/Direct Connect.
Patch & vulnerability management, hardening
  • Cycle: discover assets, scan (Qualys/Nessus), prioritise (CVSS, exploitability, asset criticality), test in UAT, change approval, deploy in windows, verify, rollback plan, report.
  • CIS Benchmarks for hardening: disable unused services, least privilege, MFA, logging, baseline configuration.
IaC, automation, monitoring
  • Terraform: declarative, provider, state file, plan/apply. Ansible: agentless, YAML playbooks, idempotent.
  • Monitoring: SolarWinds, Zabbix, PRTG, Splunk (logs and SIEM). Know the difference between metrics, logs and traces, and between alerting and incident management (ITIL).
BFSI context
  • RBI guidelines on IT governance, risk, controls and assurance; RBI Cyber Security Framework; data localisation; business continuity; SOC and DC/DR drills; ITIL (incident, problem, change).

Tricks and tips

Error spotting
  • Check subject-verb agreement first, then tense, articles, prepositions, pronoun case.
  • "Each, every, either, neither" take singular verbs. "One of the" + plural noun + singular verb.
  • Mixed conditionals and parallel structure are frequent traps.
Para jumbles and cloze
  • Find the opening sentence (introduces the topic, no pronoun without a noun) and the closing one (conclusion).
  • Link pairs using pronouns, connectors (however, thus), and chronology.
  • In cloze, read the full passage first, then fill by grammar before meaning.
Reading comprehension
  • Read the questions first, skim the passage for the main idea, then go back for details.
  • Eliminate extreme words (always, never, only) unless the passage says so.
Vocabulary and idioms
  • Learn 10 words a day with a synonym and an antonym; revise weekly.
  • Learn idioms in sentences, not as isolated lists.
Voice and speech
  • Active to passive: object becomes subject, verb becomes be + past participle.
  • Indirect speech: shift tense back one step, change pronouns and time words (now to then, today to that day).
Exam strategy (100 minutes shared)
  • Spend about 20 minutes on English (25 marks), 70+ on Professional Knowledge, 10 for review.
  • Attempt your strongest technical topics first; check whether there is negative marking in the official notice.
  • Take at least 5 full timed mocks in the last two weeks.

Banking and financial awareness

Key RBI and monetary terms
  • Repo rate: rate at which RBI lends short-term to banks. Reverse repo: rate at which RBI borrows from banks. SDF/MSF: standing deposit and marginal standing facility. Check the current rates on rbi.org.in, as they change.
  • CRR: share of deposits (NDTL) banks keep with RBI as cash. SLR: share kept in liquid assets (cash, gold, government securities).
  • MCLR / EBLR: internal and external benchmark-linked lending rates.
  • Bank Rate: long-term rate at which RBI lends without collateral; penal rates are linked to it.
Asset quality and regulation
  • NPA: loan overdue for more than 90 days. Categories: Sub-standard, Doubtful, Loss.
  • SARFAESI Act: lets banks seize secured assets without court. IBC: time-bound insolvency resolution via NCLT.
  • Basel III: capital (CET1, CRAR), liquidity (LCR, NSFR) and leverage norms. PCA: prompt corrective action for weak banks.
  • KYC / AML: customer identification and anti-money-laundering rules. DICGC: insures deposits up to Rs 5 lakh per depositor per bank.
  • PSL: priority sector lending targets (agriculture, MSME, education, housing, weaker sections).
Payments and digital banking
  • NPCI runs UPI, IMPS, RuPay, NACH, FASTag. RTGS is for large-value real-time transfers (min Rs 2 lakh); NEFT runs in 24x7 batches.
  • CBDC (e-rupee), Account Aggregator, ONDC, Open banking APIs, SWIFT, Aadhaar-enabled payments (AePS), BBPS.
  • Cyber rules: RBI Cyber Security Framework, IT Governance directions, Digital Personal Data Protection Act, CERT-In incident reporting.
Banking terms to know cold
  • CASA, NIM, CRAR, GNPA/NNPA, PCR, haircut, lien, hypothecation, mortgage, pledge, escrow, factoring, forfeiting, letter of credit, bank guarantee, overdraft, cash credit, bancassurance, ECS, nostro/vostro, treasury bills, call money, CBLO/TREPS, certificate of deposit, commercial paper, ARC, SRs.

Speed math kit

  • Square ending in 5: n5² = n(n+1) then 25. Example: 85² = (8x9) 72, then 25, so 7225.
  • Multiply by 11: add neighbouring digits. 63 x 11 = 6 (6+3) 3 = 693.
  • Fractions to percent: 1/2=50, 1/3=33.33, 1/4=25, 1/5=20, 1/6=16.67, 1/7=14.28, 1/8=12.5, 1/9=11.11, 1/10=10, 1/11=9.09, 1/12=8.33.
  • Successive change a% then b%: a + b + ab/100. Two discounts of 10% and 20% = 28% total.
  • Compound interest: for 2 years at r%, CI - SI = P(r/100)².
  • Time and work: take LCM of days as total work; efficiency = work/days.
  • Average speed for equal distances: 2xy/(x+y).
  • Profit/loss: SP = CP x (100 + P%)/100. Use ratios, not decimals.

RAID, CVSS, uptime
  • RAID 0 = speed, no redundancy. RAID 1 = mirror. RAID 5 = 1 disk fault (min 3). RAID 6 = 2 disk faults (min 4). RAID 10 = mirror + stripe (min 4).
  • CVSS: 0.1-3.9 Low, 4.0-6.9 Medium, 7.0-8.9 High, 9.0-10.0 Critical.
  • Uptime: 99.9% = about 8.8 hours downtime a year; 99.99% = about 53 minutes; 99.999% = about 5 minutes.
  • OSI layers: Physical, Data Link, Network, Transport, Session, Presentation, Application.
